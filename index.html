Unnecessary disposal
Phones, chargers and earphones get thrown away even when they can still be repaired.

          
Repair feels expensive
People assume replacing is cheaper because they cannot see a repair estimate up front.

          
Poor recycling
Old devices end up in regular waste or informal channels instead of authorised recyclers.

          
Pollution & lost materials
Improper disposal releases hazardous substances and wastes recoverable metals.

        

      


      

        

          

          
          
          
          

        

        

          
How TechCycle works
One platform for the whole device lifecycle

          
    
            
  1. Check. Describe the device and fault. The engine scores repairability from 0 to 100 and estimates cost, savings and CO₂e.
    2. 
            
  2. Repair. Find a nearby partner on the map and book a doorstep pickup or drop-off. Every request gets a trackable ID.
    3. 
            
  3. Reuse. Buy tested spare parts and refurbished devices, or list your own, instead of buying new.
    4. 
            
  4. Recycle. When repair is not worth it, wipe your data, send the device to an authorised recycler and get a disposal certificate.
    5.

         In context
        
            
        The world generated 62 million tonnes of e-waste in 2022, and only 22.3% was documented as formally collected and recycled (Global E-waste Monitor 2024). Every repaired device and every formally recycled one moves that number in the right direction.
        
            
        Assumptions used in these estimates
              
          
                
        • A completed repair is assumed to replace the purchase of one new device of the same price segment.
          • 
                
        • CO₂e avoided = the device's manufacturing footprint × 0.9 (10% is allowed for replacement parts).
          • 
                
        • Indicative footprints: smartphone 60 kg, laptop 250 kg, tablet 100 kg, earphones 6 kg, charger 2 kg CO₂e. These are order-of-magnitude values from manufacturer product environmental reports and LCA literature.
          • 
                
        • Money saved = new device price − final repair cost (or the midpoint of the estimate).
          • 
                
        • Recycling counts device weight as e-waste diverted to formal channels, but no CO₂e credit is claimed.
          •
